Over time, however, branches can grow toward roofs, windows, driveways and utility areas. Dead limbs can develop. Crowded branches can compete for space. Storms can leave damaged growth behind.

Do Not Wait for a Major Problem

Tree maintenance is often easier when small structural issues are addressed before branches become large, heavy or difficult to reach.

If a large limb is hanging, cracked or positioned over a structure, avoid working underneath it yourself. Have the situation assessed by an appropriately qualified tree professional.

What Professional Tree Trimming Should Look Like

Good pruning is deliberate. The objective is to make useful cuts while maintaining the tree's natural form and long-term structure.

Assess Before Cutting

A tree should be evaluated before branches are removed. Species, age, condition, branch attachment and surrounding structures all matter.

Make Selective Cuts

Removing every branch that looks inconvenient can weaken a tree. Professional pruning focuses on branches that have a clear reason for removal.

Avoid Unnecessary Topping

Cutting large portions from the top of a tree is not a substitute for proper pruning. Tree work should respect the natural structure of the canopy.

Frequently Asked Questions About Tree Trimming

How often should trees be trimmed in Alpharetta?

There is no universal schedule. Tree species, age, health, structure, location and pruning objectives all affect how often trimming may be appropriate.

Is tree trimming the same as tree pruning?

The terms are often used interchangeably. In professional tree care, pruning usually refers to the deliberate removal of selected branches to improve structure, health, clearance or safety.

Can tree trimming damage a tree?

Yes. Excessive cutting or poorly placed cuts can create long-term problems. Good pruning should remove only branches that have a clear reason for removal.

Should branches touching my roof be trimmed?

Branches touching or growing over a roof should be evaluated. Selective pruning can often improve clearance while preserving the majority of the healthy canopy.

Is tree topping recommended?

Tree topping is generally not a proper substitute for professional pruning. Selective cuts should be used to address specific structural or clearance concerns.

Should I trim a tree near a power line myself?

No. Tree work around electrical lines can be extremely hazardous. Such situations should be evaluated and handled by appropriately qualified professionals.

When should I call an arborist?

Consider an arborist evaluation when you notice substantial deadwood, storm damage, unusual decline, structural concerns, large branches near structures or a tree that needs significant pruning.
